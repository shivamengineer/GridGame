# CDC Simulator Game  

## Description: 
> This is a game built on a hexagon grid. The player will build and expand their city, increasing the population and collecting more and more resources. Though eventually, Covid-19 or some other disease will spread to/throughout the player's city, and the player must respond to it. The player may prepare how they choose for the disease, though the disease may adapt as it spreads, and the citizens may be unhappy with the player's choices. This game has many similarities to Civilization VI at the moment, though is real time instead of turn based, and will have more of a focus on stopping the spread of a disease.
>> Hopefully this game can be somewhat educational about the development of vaccines and the spread of diseases, though I am not an expert, so please reach out if you have any ideas! I developed a prototype for this game on a team at a hackathon in early March 2020, but this game hopefully can go into a lot more detail about the decisions that need to be made during a pandemic.
>> Game has inspiration from Civilization VI and Plague Inc
